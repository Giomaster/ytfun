# Channel Brief

## Positioning

The channel should be explicit about its job: trend intelligence for people who want to understand what is catching attention now.

Working brand direction:

- Name: Radar de Agora
- Promise: O que o mundo esta assistindo agora.
- Format: original videos that explain trends, with Shorts cut from our own uploads.
- Tone: fast, curious, direct, lightly witty, never random.

## Content Pillars

- Internet moments: what is spreading and why.
- Creator economy: formats, hooks, retention tricks, and viral mechanics.
- Tech and AI culture: launches, demos, reactions, and adoption waves.
- Entertainment signals: memes, games, music-adjacent moments, and pop culture.
- Business of attention: what is monetizable and why.

## Production Template

Long-form source video:

- Original commentary, analysis, and/or curated narrative.
- One clear trend thesis.
- Several self-contained moments that can become Shorts.

Shorts:

- Cut from our own long-form videos.
- Visual mode: none by default.
- Framing: foreground preserved over blurred vertical background.
- Audio: original source audio when useful; optional light music/SFX only if timing-safe.

## Thumbnail Direction

Long-form thumbnails should be bold but clean:

- 16:9 custom thumbnail generated from a real frame of the video.
- Translucent circular/radial foreground glow with the center near the lower-right corner.
- Transparent edges, vivid but controlled color.
- Optional curiosity marker only: `???`, one short word, or one visual emoji.
- Avoid poster layouts, channel badges, tiny UI, meme-wall chaos, and heavy text.

Shorts thumbnails:

- YouTube currently does not support uploading a custom thumbnail for Shorts like regular videos.
- Choose a strong frame from the Short after upload.
- If needed later, generate an intentional cover frame inside the Short, but avoid this for the current no-visual MVP.
