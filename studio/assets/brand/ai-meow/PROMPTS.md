# AI Meow — prompts e origem das artes

Modo: **gerador integrado de imagens**. As adaptações criativas usaram a logo e a cena aprovadas como referências. Exportações nas medidas exatas e recortes centrais foram feitos com o utilitário macOS `sips`; os wallpapers ampliados não acrescentam detalhe nativo.

Os PNGs originais estão em `originais/`. A marca adotada é a primeira AI Meow, com o I à direita. A variação com I central foi descartada para a identidade final. Os prompts abaixo preservam o texto enviado ao gerador; metas de resolução ou composição no prompt não significam que o gerador retornou essas medidas exatas.

## Logo aprovada

Referência/resultado preservado: [originais/avatar-aprovado-1254x1254.png](originais/avatar-aprovado-1254x1254.png). Geracao inicial; o resultado aprovado tem A-gato e I separado a direita.

```text
Use case: logo-brand.
Brand name: "AI Meow".
Primary request: a NEW original logo combining artificial intelligence and a cat. The user wants a clean, simple, memorable icon with few details and mostly right angles.
Asset type: square social profile logo for YouTube, Facebook, TikTok and Kwai.
Design: a minimal geometric cat-head glyph cleverly integrated with an AI monogram. Use a compact, balanced silhouette made from a few bold rectangular strokes and two angular ear tips. The letter A can suggest the cat's head/ears and the I can be integrated as one vertical stroke or a simple eye-like slot; the result must read first as a clever CAT + AI symbol, not as ordinary letters with a cat illustration pasted on. Use negative space carefully. Only two small geometric eyes if needed; no nose, mouth, whiskers, fur or complex facial features. Cat ears and a memorable silhouette are the main cues.
Style: refined minimal vector-like brand mark; flat solid electric cyan on perfectly uniform midnight navy. Thick consistent strokes, mostly horizontal and vertical edges with a few precise diagonals for ears. Friendly, intelligent and slightly playful. Strong optical balance and recognizable at 32px.
Composition: ONE centered icon filling around 55–60% of a square canvas, generous padding suitable for circular avatar cropping.
Constraints: no human, no mascot illustration, no detailed robot, no circuits, no gradients, no glow, no shadow, no 3D, no texture, no headphones, no decorative frame, no mockup, no alternate options on one page. No wordmark or additional text. If letter shapes are used, order is A-I, never I-A. One sophisticated simple logo, not a generic cat clipart.
```
